ABOUT THESE ESTIMATES · SYNTHETIC CALIBRATION

This model was trained on synthetic pothos data (2,000 simulated plants over 104 weeks). Its reported accuracy and 90% prediction interval measure performance on simulated plants and may not reflect real-world accuracy. The healthy-plant simulation assumes zero leaf loss and estimates new leaves using an average node spacing of 1.2 inches per leaf.
