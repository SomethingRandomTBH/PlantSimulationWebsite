About Plant Simulation

Our project is designed to use machine learning to understand the development process of common house plants, translating environmental telemetry into calibrated growth forecasts and actionable horticultural care plans.

SIMULATION COHORT

Physiology-Guided Synthetic Data

Models Golden Pothos (Epipremnum aureum) across 2,000 simulated plants tracked over 104 weekly intervals (208,000 observations), incorporating light saturation, thermal curves, pot root capacity, and maturity ramps.

CONFORMAL RF

Calibrated Uncertainty

Uses a 100-tree Random Forest regressor paired with split-conformal calibration on held-out plants to provide a 90% prediction interval (±0.19 inches) alongside 7-day, 14-day, and 28-day sequential forecasts.

MULTIMODAL AI

Gemini Botanical Diagnostics

Combines quantitative model forecasts and optional plant photographs to generate structured horticultural assessments, plausibility checks, and seasonal care guides.
