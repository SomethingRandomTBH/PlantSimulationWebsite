About These Estimates

This model was trained on synthetic pothos data. Its reported accuracy measures performance on simulated plants and may not reflect real-world accuracy. The healthy-plant simulation assumes no leaf loss. Predictions are estimates, not guaranteed biological outcomes. Lux is also not a accurate way to describe incoming light for a plant, however it can be used as a rough estimate.
